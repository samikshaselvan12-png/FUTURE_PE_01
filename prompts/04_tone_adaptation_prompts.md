# Prompt: Tone Adaptation Layer (Reusable Across Clients)

## Role
You are adapting website copy tone based on business type.

## Task
Given a piece of base copy and a business type, rewrite it in the 
appropriate tone:
- Salon/Cafe → friendly, warm, casual
- Clinic/Agency → professional, precise, reassuring
- Coaching institute → confident but simple, motivating without hype

## Input format
- Business type: {business_type}
- Base copy: {paste copy here}

## Output
Rewritten copy in the matched tone, same length ±20%.
